How to Donate to the Tor Project

The Tor Project is a non-profit organization dedicated to advancing human rights and online privacy through free and open-source anonymity technologies. Private individuals can support the Tor Project's mission through a variety of flexible donation methods, including traditional payment systems like credit cards and PayPal, numerous cryptocurrencies, direct bank transfers, mailed checks, stock transfers, and employer matching programs.

Credit Cards and PayPal

Individual donors can make one-time or recurring monthly donations directly through the Tor Project's official donation portal. The platform accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover) as well as PayPal. Setting up a monthly recurring contribution helps provide predictable funding for ongoing network maintenance and software development.

Cryptocurrency

For donors who prefer privacy-preserving or decentralized payment methods, the Tor Project accepts a wide range of cryptocurrencies. Supported digital currencies include:

  • Bitcoin (BTC)
  • Ethereum (ETH)
  • Monero (XMR)
  • Litecoin (LTC)
  • Zcash (ZEC)
  • Dai (DAI)

Cryptocurrency donations can be sent directly to the official wallet addresses listed on the Tor Project's donate page.

Bank Transfers and Physical Checks

Donors can make contributions via direct wire transfer or ACH transfer. The Tor Project provides its banking details upon request for those wishing to send funds directly from their bank accounts.

Physical checks denominated in US Dollars can also be mailed directly to the Tor Project's postal address. Checks should be made payable to "The Tor Project, Inc."

Stock and Mutual Fund Donations

Gifting appreciated securities—such as stocks, bonds, or mutual funds—is a tax-efficient way for individuals to support the organization. Donating stock directly allows contributors to avoid paying capital gains taxes while receiving a charitable tax deduction for the full fair market value of the asset.

Donor-Advised Funds (DAF) and Employer Matching

Individuals with Donor-Advised Funds can recommend grants directly to the Tor Project. Additionally, many companies offer matching gift programs that double or triple charitable contributions made by their employees. Donors can check with their employer's human resources department to submit a matching gift request.

Tax-Deductible Status

The Tor Project, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in the United States. Contributions made by US taxpayers are generally tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law. Donors who provide their contact details receive an official receipt for tax filing purposes.
