How Tor Snowflake Usage Has Grown in Recent Years

Tor Snowflake has experienced unprecedented growth in recent years, evolving from an experimental pluggable transport into one of the Tor Project’s primary anti-censorship tools. Driven by escalating internet restrictions, major geopolitical conflicts, and an expanding global network of volunteer proxies, Snowflake's daily active user base and proxy network have scaled rapidly to help individuals maintain open access to the free web.

Snowflake operates by using WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication) to route user traffic through temporary, volunteer-hosted proxies created via browser extensions or web pages. Because these connections appear as regular peer-to-peer audio or video traffic, censors find them significantly harder to identify and block compared to traditional static Tor bridges.

The primary catalyst for Snowflake's massive surge was the escalation of state-sponsored internet censorship starting in 2022. Following Russia's invasion of Ukraine and subsequent crackdowns on independent media, hundreds of thousands of Russian users turned to Snowflake to bypass state firewalls. Later that same year, widespread protests in Iran led the government to implement sweeping internet blackouts and block standard VPNs and Tor bridges. In response, Iranian users adopted Snowflake en masse, causing the daily connected user count to skyrocket from just a few thousand to hundreds of thousands within a matter of weeks.

To support this massive influx of users, the Snowflake proxy ecosystem expanded concurrently. Public awareness campaigns by digital rights organizations encouraged internet users worldwide to install the Snowflake browser extension or run standalone Docker containers. This mobilization caused the number of active, volunteer-operated proxies to increase tenfold, ensuring that censored users consistently had access to available connection paths.

Integration into popular consumer privacy applications further accelerated adoption. The inclusion of Snowflake as a default pluggable transport in the official Tor Browser, Orbot for Android and iOS, and integrations within other privacy-focused browsers simplified the connection process for non-technical users.

Today, Snowflake remains a vital lifeline in the fight against digital censorship. Continued enhancements in traffic obfuscation, increased server capacity, and sustained community participation have established Snowflake as an essential and resilient component of modern internet freedom infrastructure.
