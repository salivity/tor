Does Tor Accept Cryptocurrency Donations?

The Tor Project actively accepts cryptocurrency donations to support its mission of providing free, open-source software for online privacy and censorship resistance. Supporters worldwide can contribute using major digital assets like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Monero directly to the organization. This article covers the specific cryptocurrencies Tor accepts, how to make a donation, and why digital currency plays an important role in funding Tor’s global operations.

Supported Cryptocurrencies

The Tor Project supports a wide variety of cryptocurrencies to make giving accessible to users globally. The accepted assets generally include:

  • Bitcoin (BTC)
  • Ethereum (ETH)
  • Monero (XMR)
  • Litecoin (LTC)
  • Zcash (ZEC)
  • Dai (DAI) and other stablecoins

Privacy-centric coins like Monero and Zcash are particularly popular among Tor users due to their alignment with Tor's core philosophy of digital privacy and untraceable transactions.

How Tor Crypto Donations Work

To donate, users can visit the official Tor Project donation page. For standard donations, Tor lists direct wallet addresses as well as integrations with crypto-philanthropy processors to handle transactions and generate tax receipts where applicable.

Because The Tor Project is an officially recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in the United States, cryptocurrency donations are often tax-deductible for U.S. taxpayers. Users seeking a receipt can provide their email address during the checkout process, while those wishing to remain anonymous can send funds directly to the designated public wallet addresses.

Why the Tor Project Relies on Crypto

Cryptocurrency donations provide several distinct advantages for an organization dedicated to internet freedom:

  • Censorship Resistance: Users living under authoritarian regimes or in countries with strict financial controls can donate without facing banking blocks or political interference.
  • Global Accessibility: Cross-border transactions occur quickly without exorbitant international wire fees or currency conversion barriers.
  • Financial Privacy: Donors who require anonymity can contribute using privacy-focused assets without disclosing personal banking details.

Donating Safely

Because malicious actors frequently set up fraudulent domains to steal funds, always verify that you are on the official Tor Project website before copying any wallet addresses or submitting transactions. Using Tor Browser to access their official .onion address provides an additional layer of security to ensure the wallet addresses have not been spoofed.
