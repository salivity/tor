How the Tor Project Manages Financial Transparency

The Tor Project maintains financial transparency by operating as an open-source 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, subjecting its finances to rigorous independent audits, and publishing all major financial records directly to the public. Through annual financial reports, IRS Form 990 filings, and detailed grant disclosures, the organization provides full visibility into its funding sources, operational expenditures, and executive compensation.

501(c)(3) Non-Profit Status and Public Tax Filings

As a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization in the United States, the Tor Project is legally mandated to disclose its financial activities. Every year, it files IRS Form 990, an exhaustive public document that details:

  • Total revenue broken down by grants, contributions, and program service revenue.
  • Itemized expenses, including software development, community outreach, and administrative overhead.
  • Compensation details for officers, directors, key employees, and the highest-paid contractors.

Independent Annual Financial Audits

Because the Tor Project receives federal and international grants, it is subject to standard independent financial audits, including Single Audits under US Uniform Guidance regulations. These audits are conducted by external, accredited accounting firms to verify that all funds are tracked, allocated correctly, and compliant with standard accounting principles. The Tor Project publishes these full, unedited auditor reports on its website alongside its tax filings.

Publication of Annual Reports and Financial Statements

Beyond mandatory tax filings, the Tor Project creates and distributes comprehensive annual reports to make its financial health easily understandable to non-accountants. These reports provide visual breakdowns of:

  • Income distribution from diverse sources, including government agencies, private foundations, corporate sponsorships, and individual donors.
  • Programmatic spending across specific core initiatives, such as censorship circumvention, network infrastructure, and user privacy tools.
  • Strategic objectives and corresponding budget allocations for upcoming fiscal years.

Public Disclosure of Funding Sources

To maintain trust with a user base that prioritizes privacy and security, the Tor Project explicitly lists its major donors and project sponsors. This open reporting addresses community scrutiny regarding the balance between government-backed research grants and private individual donations, ensuring users know exactly which entities contribute to the development of the Tor network.

Open Access to Historical Records

All of Tor's financial documents, spanning back to its incorporation, are hosted in an open-access repository on the Tor Project website. Anyone—including developers, security researchers, journalists, and everyday users—can freely download and examine historical Form 990s, audit letters, and financial statements without registration or approval.
